Purchase Pricing & Profit Calculator

Compare five supplier offers by landed cost, target profit, and suggested selling price.

Purchase price

Each offer adjusts the same list price independently, before tax.

Tax and exchange rate

Enter the number of selling-side units per one purchase-side unit. Use 1 when no conversion is needed.

Shipping and other costs

Use the same units as the list price. If a charge is quoted in selling-side units, divide it by the exchange rate before entering it here. Use costs for the same item or batch as the list price.

Add packing, customs clearance, duties, or handling for this item or batch.

Net revenue targets

These two inputs set your target profit above landed cost. Enter the fixed amount in selling-side units and use zero for either component you do not need.

How prices are calculated
  1. Apply one supplier offer to the list price.
  2. Multiply the adjusted purchase price by the purchase tax rate.
  3. Add adjusted price, purchase tax, shipping, and other costs. Multiply the subtotal by the exchange rate to get landed cost.
  4. Multiply landed cost by Net revenue 1 and add Net revenue 2 to get target profit.
  5. Add landed cost and target profit to get the suggested selling price.
